How to run a session

Scan to make training orders

autobagger-barcode-printer-picker-t.vercel.app
1

Scan the code. Tap Create.

Pick a preset. Type how many orders.

2

On the iPad, set status to Training.

Customer SHIPBOTS. Then tap Single Order or Multi Item Batch.

3

Pick the candy into a tote.

Scan each wrapper. Do not print a label.

4

Done? Walk away.

The cart resets itself in 30 min. Candy goes back, orders close.
